A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Good to know

How many stickers are included in each pack?＋

The Regular pack includes 64 stickers, the Large pack includes 128 stickers, and the Super pack includes 256 stickers. Every pack includes 16 different designs.

How much does each pack cost?＋

The Regular pack is $9.99, the Large pack is $17.99 (Save 10%), and the Super pack is $26.99 (Best Value · Save 32.5%). Savings are compared with equivalent quantities of Regular packs. Shipping cost is calculated separately based on your location.

Can I order from outside Indonesia?＋

Yes. Worldwide shipping is available. Please contact us via WhatsApp to confirm shipping details for your location.

Can I choose the design and name?＋

Yes. You can choose your preferred design, pack size, and the name you would like to customize.

How can I place an order?＋

Choose your preferred design and pack size, then contact us via WhatsApp. We will guide you through the ordering process.
